**What was taken from FreeBSD's tcx** (`nd_tc956x.h`): every register offset and field it uses (`if_tcxreg.h`), the QCA8081 and MII constants, the speed table, and these functions with the same constants and order: `tcx_chip_init` (TAMAP entry 0 and the MSI generators' clock, then reset), `tcx_msigen_running`, `tcx_mac_running`, `tcx_mdio_xfer` and its clause 22/45 wrappers, the PHY's MMD window, `tcx_phy_serdes_fifo`, `tcx_phy_fix_advert`, `tcx_phy_set_media`, `tcx_phy_resolve_pause`, `tcx_phy_poll`, `tcx_mac_set_link`, the XPCS viewport (`tcx_xpcs_select`/`read`/`write`/`update`), `tcx_sp_sel_speed`, `tcx_pma_init`, `tcx_xpcs_config`, `tcx_serdes_config`, `tcx_link_apply`, `tcx_mac_start`, `tcx_attach_pre`'s station address read and cold-init choice, `tcx_hash_maddr` with `ether_crc32_be`, `tcx_set_filter`, `tcx_flow_thresholds`, `tcx_init`, `tcx_stop`, `tcx_intr_enable`/`disable`, `tcx_intr`'s status handling, `tcx_tx_want_ioc`, `tcx_txd_encap` (without TSO), `tcx_txd_flush`, `tcx_txd_credits_update`, `tcx_rx_want_ioc`, `tcx_rxd_refill`, `tcx_rxd_flush`, `tcx_rxd_available` and `tcx_rxd_pkt_get` (as one ring walk) and `tcx_rx_csum`.

**What changed:** `bus_read_4`/`bus_write_4` on the newbus resources became the includer's `ND_TC956X_READ`/`WRITE`/`BRIDGE_WRITE`; `DELAY`/`pause_sbt` became `ND_TC956X_DELAY`, `device_printf` `ND_TC956X_LOG`; errno results became booleans or enumerations; iflib's callbacks became plain functions over the core's own ring indices (the IOKit driver keeps the mbufs); `iflib_link_state_change` became a returned event; `ifmedia` subtypes became Mb/s; `if_foreach_llmaddr` became `nd_tc956x_hash_add` over the list IONetworkingFamily passes; the checksum results are an enumeration the driver turns into `setChecksumResult`; `hw.tcx.cold_init` and the moderation sysctls are fields (and boot-args in the driver); the receive buffer size is fixed at 2048 (no jumbo frames); function 1 waiting for function 0 is the driver's (FreeBSD refuses to attach).

**Not taken:** TSO (`tcx_txd_encap`'s context descriptor and split header: IONetworkingFamily hands the driver only the MSS, `mbuf_get_tso_requested`), jumbo frames (`tcx_mtu_set`, `tcx_rx_bufsz`), the MMC PAUSE watch for iflib's watchdog (`tcx_timer`), the sysctls, iflib's attach plumbing and `ifdi_*` methods.

Linux's `stmmac`/`dwmac-tc956x`/`tc956x_pci` (GPL-2.0) and Toshiba's TC9564 host driver were not used here; FreeBSD's port consulted them for register facts only (its notes).
